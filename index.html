3. Safety Tips

  • Sender ka complete email address carefully check karein.
  • Unexpected links par click karne se pehle domain verify karein.
  • OTP, password, PIN ya CVV kisi ke saath share na karein.
  • Suspicious message ko official support channel se verify karein.
  • Multi-factor authentication enable rakhein.
